# Cairn User Guide

Cairn will be a calm little habit tracker. You will open it for under a minute a day, tap what you did, and see small progress add up. It is designed to forgive missed days, keep your information private and stay out of your way.

This guide will grow with each step of the app. Right now it is only a placeholder.

## Nothing to use yet

There is nothing to use yet. The first usable version will have a Today screen, where you check in. After that, Cairn comes to your phone.

## What will be here

As the app grows, this guide will explain, in plain words:

- How to add your first few habits.
- How to check in with one tap.
- How to read your progress.
- How to do your short weekly review.
- How to put Cairn on your phone and your computer.
- How to keep a copy of your data.

Check back as Cairn grows.
