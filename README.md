# Cairn

A calm personal habit and growth tracker. Each check-in adds a stone to the pile.

Cairn is an installable web app (PWA) for a PC and an iPhone. It aims to take under a minute a day: three habits at most to start, one tap to check in, forgiving weekly targets, and honest views of slow progress. Your data stays on your device first and can be exported at any time.

Status: early development. See [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md) once it exists, and the full specification in [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md).
