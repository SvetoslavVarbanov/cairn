# Code of Conduct

Cairn is a calm project, and we want the community around it to feel the same way. This is a short version in our own words. For a longer, well-known example that inspired it, see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Our promise

Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews should be able to do so without being treated badly. This applies to everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people who are not programmers.
- Give feedback that is specific and helpful, and take feedback with grace.
- Respect other people's time and privacy.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or exclusion.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Unwanted attention, spam or repeated pestering.
- Anything else that would make a reasonable person feel unwelcome or unsafe.

## Reporting a problem

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, please tell the maintainer privately. Open a short issue that only says you would like to report a conduct problem privately, without any details, and the maintainer will get in touch. Please do not describe conduct problems in public issues.

Reports are handled in confidence, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainer will read the report, listen to the people involved, and decide on a fair response. Depending on what happened, that may be a friendly reminder, a warning, removal of comments or contributions, a temporary pause, or a permanent ban from the project.

Thank you for helping keep this a kind place.
